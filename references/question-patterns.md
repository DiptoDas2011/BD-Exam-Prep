# Question patterns (প্রশ্নের ধরন)

> **Maintainer note:** mark distributions and time limits differ by subject, class and year. Items marked **VERIFY** must be confirmed against the board's published marks distribution before you rely on them. Update this file when you do, and put the year in the "Last verified" line at the bottom.

## 1. সৃজনশীল প্রশ্ন (CQ) structure

One CQ = one **উদ্দীপক** (stimulus: a short passage, scenario, diagram description, data table, poem excerpt or experiment) + four parts:

| Part | Name | Marks | What it tests | Typical stems |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ক | জ্ঞানমূলক (Knowledge) | 1 | Recall of a fact or definition | কী? কাকে বলে? কত? কোনটি? |
| খ | অনুধাবনমূলক (Comprehension) | 2 | Explaining in own words | কেন? ব্যাখ্যা কর। বুঝিয়ে লেখ। |
| গ | প্রয়োগমূলক (Application) | 3 | Applying a concept or formula to the stimulus | নির্ণয় কর। উদ্দীপকের আলোকে ... দেখাও। |
| ঘ | উচ্চতর দক্ষতামূলক (Higher-order) | 4 | Analysis, evaluation, judgement | বিশ্লেষণ কর। মূল্যায়ন কর। তুমি কি একমত? যুক্তি দাও। |

Total: **10 marks per CQ**. In many SSC subjects a student answers a fixed number of CQs out of a larger set (commonly 7 of 10 in some subjects) **VERIFY per subject**.

### CQ writing rules
- ক and খ may use the textbook directly. গ and ঘ **must depend on the উদ্দীপক**; if the stimulus is removed, গ and ঘ should become unanswerable.
- The stimulus is new, realistic and Bangladeshi in context (village, city, school, market, river, festival) where natural.
- গ is usually computation or a direct application (one clear path to the answer).
- ঘ requires a judgement: compare two cases, decide whether a claim is correct, predict and justify, or evaluate a decision. The model answer must state a **conclusion with reasons**, not just facts.
- Each part is answerable independently where possible, so a mistake in গ does not make ঘ impossible. When ঘ must reuse গ's result, say so in the model answer and give the marking note.
- Provide a **model answer** and a **marking note** (what earns each mark) for every part.

### CQ output template (chat)

```
সৃজনশীল প্রশ্ন ১ | অধ্যায়: <name> | বোর্ড-স্টাইল (মৌলিক)

উদ্দীপক: <2-5 sentences>

ক. <question> (১)
খ. <question> (২)
গ. <question> (৩)
ঘ. <question> (৪)
```

Answer block (shown after the attempt):

```
উত্তর ১
ক. <answer>
খ. <answer> [marking: 1 for idea, 1 for reason]
গ. <steps and result> [marking: formula 1, substitution 1, result with unit 1]
ঘ. <analysis and conclusion> [marking: ...]
```

## 2. বহুনির্বাচনি প্রশ্ন (MCQ)

1 mark each, four options (ক, খ, গ, ঘ), exactly one correct. Commonly 30 questions in 30 minutes for the SSC MCQ part **VERIFY**.

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| সাধারণ বহুনির্বাচনি | One question stem, four options |
| বহুপদী সমাপ্তিসূচক | Three statements (i, ii, iii); options are combinations such as "i ও ii", "i ও iii", "ii ও iii", "i, ii ও iii" |
| অভিন্ন তথ্যভিত্তিক | One shared stimulus followed by 2 questions that depend on it |

### MCQ writing rules
- One clearly correct option. Distractors must be plausible (common mistakes, near values, unit errors), not silly.
- Do not use "সবগুলো" or "কোনোটিই নয়" as a habit.
- Spread correct answers across ক খ গ ঘ roughly evenly.
- Numerical MCQ: check that the distractors come from realistic slips (wrong formula, missed unit conversion).
- Give a one-line explanation for each answer.

## 4. Subject notes

- **গণিত / উচ্চতর গণিত:** CQ stimulus is often a geometric figure, a table or a real-life situation. Part গ asks for a calculation; ঘ asks for a proof, a multi-step argument or a decision. Show every step in the model answer.
- **পদার্থবিজ্ঞান / রসায়ন / জীববিজ্ঞান:** stimulus is a scenario, lab observation or data. Include units and significant figures. Chemistry: balanced equations. Biology: diagram-based stems work well.
- **বাংলা ১ম/২য় পত্র:** CQ stimuli link a poem or prose piece to a new passage. Grammar and composition (ব্যাকরণ ও নির্মিতি) follow different formats; ask the student which part they need.
- **English 1st / 2nd paper:** the format is not the standard CQ. Use reading comprehension, grammar-in-context, writing (paragraph, letter, email, composition) tasks. **VERIFY** current format.
- **ICT, বাংলাদেশ ও বিশ্বপরিচয়, ধর্ম, কৃষি, গার্হস্থ্য বিজ্ঞান, ইত্যাদি:** standard CQ and MCQ; check the chapter file for key terms.
