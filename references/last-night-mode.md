# Last-night mode (শেষ রাতের প্ল্যান)

Pick the plan by hours left. Keep sessions realistic: tired students retain little after the first 60-90 minutes.

## 3 hours or more

| Step | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Revision sheet for the syllabus chapters: key terms, formulas, diagrams to remember, 5 likely CQ angles per chapter |
| 2 | 30 min | Timed MCQ set (20 questions, 20 minutes), then review |
| 3 | 45 min | 3 CQs, written by the student, then compared with the model answers |
| 4 | 20 min | Mistake review: only the wrong answers |
| 5 | 15 min | Round 2: 10 MCQs from the weakest topics |
| 6 | 10 min | One-page morning glance sheet, then stop and sleep |

## 1 to 3 hours

1. 10-minute formula and key-term sheet for the 3 most important chapters
2. 15 MCQs, timed (15 minutes), review wrong answers
3. 2 CQs, focusing on parts গ and ঘ (they carry the most marks)
4. Morning glance sheet, then sleep

## Under 1 hour

1. 5-minute sheet: formulas, definitions and diagrams only
2. 10 MCQs from the highest-weight chapter
3. Review wrong answers; stop

## Rules for every plan

- Prefer **chapters with the most marks** and **chapters the student already half knows** over chapters they have never opened. Tonight is not the time for a brand-new chapter unless it is heavily weighted.
- Don't claim which questions "will come". Say which topics carry weight and why.
- Revision sheet format: bullets, short lines, Bangla terms matching the textbook, formulas in plain text with units.
- Morning glance sheet: maximum 10 lines.
- Close by reminding the student to sleep and eat something light. Be brief and kind.

